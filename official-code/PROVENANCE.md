## What this establishes

**The ratifying body is the Sindhi Language Authority, not the Education
Department.** The SLA is an autonomous institution of the Government of Sindh,
established under the Use of Sindhi Language Act 1972 and the Teaching,
Promotion and Use of Sindhi Language (Amendment) Act 1990. Its mandate covers
the standing and correct application of Sindhi in official documents, textbooks
and publications. For standardising a writing system, that is the more
authoritative body of the two — and it is chaired here by the Chairman of the
Authority himself.

**This is a committee standard, not one person's system.** Six named members,
unanimous agreement, convened and hosted by the Authority, certified by its
Secretary. Riaz Hussain Memon is a member of that committee — the blind expert
on it — not its sole author.

**It completes a documented process.** The 47-page standard guide records a 2014
seminar that passed three resolutions: form a committee of experts, standardise
the alphabet chart, and prepare a Sindhi braille guide book. This certificate,
dated 7 November 2016, is that committee delivering its result.

## Consequences for the project

**Correct the attribution everywhere.** We had been describing the code as
"approved by the Sindh Education Department." The document in hand says
**Sindhi Language Authority, 7 November 2016**. A separate Education Department
approval for classroom teaching may also exist — worth asking Riaz — but it is
not this certificate, and it is not what we should cite.

**This is what the liblouis submission needs.** Their maintainers ask for a
reference to official documentation of the braille code being implemented, and
prefer it deposited at `github.com/liblouis/braille-specs`. A dated certificate
from a statutory language authority, naming a six-member committee, is stronger
evidence than most existing tables carry.

**It changes the authorship question.** If the standard was ratified by a named
committee, the paper should acknowledge that committee, and we should ask Riaz
whether the other members should be informed — particularly Shahid Ahmed Memon
as convener, and the Authority itself. Digitising a standard that a government
body ratified is something they will want to know about, and their support is
worth more than their surprise.
